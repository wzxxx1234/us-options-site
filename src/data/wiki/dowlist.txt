The [[Dow Jones Industrial Average]] (DJIA) is a [[stock market index]] tracking the stock performance of 30 large companies listed on [[stock exchange]]s in the [[United States]]. The index is a [[price-weighted index]]; stocks with the highest price per share have the highest weighting in the index.<ref name=data>{{cite web | url=https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/indices/equity/dow-jones-industrial-average/#data | title=Dow Jones Industrial Average® | publisher=[[S&P Global]]}}</ref> An updated weighting of each component is available [https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-dow-jones-industrial-average-etf-trust-dia online].
